III

Domain adversarial training (DANN)

A gradient-reversal domain discriminator forces domain-invariant features. Trained jointly on CIC-IoT and Edge-IIoT, one DANN reaches 96.3–96.5% on both with no per-dataset fine-tuning — a 1–1.3 point cost over per-dataset models, bought back as cross-domain robustness.

Per-dataset model vs joint DANN
accuracy % · one DANN covers both datasets
Why two domains, not three

Adding WSN-DS breaks convergence: routing-layer features share no structure with transport flows, so no domain-invariant representation exists for the discriminator to find.

3-domain DANN failed to converge.Threat accuracy plateaued at ~55% with domain accuracy oscillating between 33% (chance) and 67%. Gradient reversal bridges related domains only.
IV

Preprocessing ablation — log normalization

The single largest accuracy gain came from preprocessing, not architecture. On Edge-IIoTset the tcp.ack_raw column (mean 1.7 billion) overwhelmed RobustScaler until a sign-preserving log transform compressed its range.
